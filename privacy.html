Privacy Policy — MyTennisApp

Effective date: 10 August 2026

MyTennisApp (“the app”) helps you record your tennis matches, opponents and practice sessions. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: the app stores your data only on your own device and does not send it anywhere.

Information the app stores

The app stores the information you choose to enter — match records, opponent details, practice sessions and the notes you add. This information is saved locally on your device, in your browser’s storage, so the app can show it back to you.

Information the app does not collect

The app has no user accounts and no server. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. Specifically, it does not:

  • send your matches, opponents or practices to us or any third party;
  • use analytics, tracking or advertising services;
  • access your contacts, camera, microphone or location;
  • require you to sign in or provide an email address.

How your data is stored and secured

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends on your device and browser. If you clear your browser’s storage, uninstall the app, or use a different device, the data on that device is removed or will not be present. You can create your own backup at any time using the app’s Data → Backup option, which saves a file to a location you choose.

Your control over your data

You are in full control. Within the app you can add, edit and delete any record, export your data to a file, and restore it from a backup. To remove all data, delete your entries or clear the app’s storage in your browser settings.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and collects no personal information from any user, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
