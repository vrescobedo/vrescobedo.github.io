# Privacy Policy — MyTennisApp

**Effective date:** 31 August 2026

MyTennisApp ("the app") helps you record your tennis matches, opponents and practice sessions. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: **the tennis data you enter stays on your own device.** The only information that ever leaves your device is **anonymous, aggregated usage statistics** collected through Cloudflare Web Analytics, described below.

## Information the app stores

The app stores the information you choose to enter — match records, opponent details, practice sessions and the notes you add. This information is saved **locally on your device**, in your browser's storage, so the app can show it back to you. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

## Anonymous usage analytics

To understand how many people use the app and which countries they visit from, the app uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics**. This is a privacy-first analytics service that:

- does **not** use cookies or any similar tracking technology;
- does **not** store your IP address (it is used only, in the moment, to derive an approximate country and is then discarded);
- does **not** identify you or track you across other websites or apps;
- collects only aggregate, non-personal information such as page views, referring sites, device type and approximate country.

This data is processed by Cloudflare, Inc. acting as our service provider. You can read about their practices in the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Information the app does *not* collect

The app has no user accounts and no server for your tennis data. Beyond the anonymous analytics described above, it does **not**:

- send your matches, opponents or practices to us or any third party;
- use advertising services or track you across other sites and apps;
- use cookies;
- access your contacts, camera, microphone or precise (GPS) location;
- require you to sign in or provide an email address.

## How your data is stored and secured

Because your tennis data stays on your device, its security depends on your device and browser. If you clear your browser's storage, uninstall the app, or use a different device, the data on that device is removed or will not be present. You can create your own backup at any time using the app's **Data → Backup** option, which saves a file to a location you choose. Analytics data is transmitted securely over HTTPS.

## Your control over your data

You are in full control of the tennis data you enter. Within the app you can add, edit and delete any record, export your data to a file, and restore it from a backup. To remove all data, delete your entries or clear the app's storage in your browser settings. The usage analytics is anonymous and aggregated, so it contains no personal records that can be tied to you individually.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The tennis data you enter stays on your device, and the usage analytics described above collects only anonymous, aggregated information from any user.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
